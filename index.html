Africa's Greatest
Safari Kingdom

Kenya is home to the world's most iconic wildlife spectacles — from the thundering Great Migration to the snow-capped peaks of Mount Kenya rising above the equator.

Spot lions, leopards, elephants, buffalos & rhinos across Kenya's legendary reserves.

Our Story

Born from the
African Soil

Tembo Trails was founded by Kenyan-born naturalist James Kariuki, who grew up watching lions on the Mara and guiding visitors through his homeland's extraordinary landscapes.

Today our team of certified guides, all native Kenyans, bring a depth of knowledge and passion that only comes from growing up alongside the wildlife. We believe the best safari is one that benefits both the traveller and the local community.

  • ✓ KWS-certified expert naturalist guides
  • ✓ Fully customised private safari itineraries
  • ✓ Community-owned conservancy partnerships
  • ✓ Carbon-offset travel options available

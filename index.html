Kenya Travel FAQs

Answers to the questions travellers ask most when planning a Kenya or East Africa trip.

What tours and services does Vianterra offer?

Vianterra offers yacht cruises and private charters, coastal day tours to Wasini Island, Mombasa, Malindi, Watamu and Diani, safaris in Tsavo East, Amboseli, the Maasai Mara and Shimba Hills, Zanzibar and East Africa getaways, accommodation booking, and tailor-made travel packages.

How much does a Wasini Island tour cost?

Wasini Island day tours start from KES 4,999 per person for Kenyan residents and $85 USD per person for non-residents. The tour includes a dhow cruise, dolphin spotting, coral reef snorkelling and a seafood lunch. Private tours start from $150 USD per person depending on group size.

Do you organise safaris from Mombasa?

Yes. We run day trips and multi-day safaris from Mombasa and the Kenyan coast, including Tsavo East, Amboseli, the Maasai Mara and Shimba Hills. Tsavo East day trips start from KES 7,500 per person for group joining, while private safaris start from $280 USD per person.

Can you arrange Zanzibar holidays?

Yes. We plan 3, 5 and 7-night Zanzibar packages from KES 55,000 per person, including Stone Town tours, spice farm visits, snorkelling, dhow cruises and beach resort stays. We can also combine Zanzibar with a Kenya safari.

How do I book a trip with Vianterra?

Message us on WhatsApp at +254 784 210 268, call the same number, or send an enquiry through the contact form. Tell us your dates, group size and interests, and we will reply with a tailored itinerary and quote.

What is the best time to visit the Kenyan coast?

The Kenyan coast is at its best during the dry seasons from December to March and July to October, when skies are clear and sea conditions are good for dhow cruises, snorkelling and beach holidays. Wildlife viewing in Tsavo and the Maasai Mara is also excellent in the dry months.
